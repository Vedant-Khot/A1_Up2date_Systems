How Email Ingestion Works

Continuous background polling & deduplication

1. Polling Cycle

The worker inspects unread emails every 60 seconds without deleting messages.

2. Read State

Messages are flagged as read only after a job record is safely created.

3. Field Parsing

Subject line becomes the Work Ref, sender becomes Client, and body becomes Issue.

4. Deduplication

Incoming emails matching existing Work Refs automatically append notes without creating duplicates.
